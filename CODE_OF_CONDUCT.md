# Code of Conduct

PCBuildSage is built by volunteers who want PC building to be less painful for everyone. We want taking part to feel the same way.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source or to PC hardware.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- Give credit, and accept feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content, or publishing anyone's private information.
- No spam or undisclosed promotion, including retailer self-promotion in profiles or reviews.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@ManishModak](https://github.com/ManishModak), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone who breaks this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
